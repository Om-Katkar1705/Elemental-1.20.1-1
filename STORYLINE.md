# 1. The Storyline

### Chapter I: The Awakening & Aeron's Camp
In ancient times, the natural realm thrived under the balance of the **Primal Elemental Core**, a divine sphere consolidating six cosmic forces: Dry Heat, Snow, Tree, Ocean, Mountain, and Nether Fire. When cataclysm shattered the Core, two colossal guardians absorbed the fragments, twisting their original mandate into tyranny and sending shockwaves of chaos across the biomes.

You awaken in this fractured wilderness with no memories, possessing only instinct. By fashioning your first primitive stone tool, your elemental potential stirs. Smoke rises on the horizon, guiding you to a quaint sanctuary: **Aeron's Camp**.

Here stands **Aeron, the Last Skywright**, an engineer hailing from the legendary Skyforge expeditions. Through an interactive exchange, Aeron reveals the catastrophe that befell his airship and charges you with seeking out the high-altitude crash site to salvage the avionics required to rebuild your wings.

---

### Chapter II: Ascent to the Skyforge & The Jetpack
Directed by your nascent Waypoint Guidance, you ascend jagged mountain cliffs or highland peaks to discover the smoldering hull of the **Crashed Skyforge Ship**.

Inside the fortified wreckage chests, you uncover four preserved **Schematic Blueprint Maps**:
1. *Pneumatic Piston Blueprint*
2. *Aerogel Core Blueprint*
3. *Element Core Blueprint*
4. *Elemental Jetpack Chassis Blueprint*

Returning these schematics to Aeron, he shares the lost craftsmanship of sky-travel. By synthesizing pistons from iron and redstone, shaping airy aerogel from soul sand and feathers, and stabilizing volatile Element Cores, you assemble the **Elemental Jetpack**. Fastening it into your dedicated harness, the thrusters roar to life—granting effortless flight without relinquishing your chest armor.

---

### Chapter III: The Desert Expedition & Eldrin the Wizard
With flight at your command, Aeron directs your compass toward coordinates thousands of blocks away across boundless dunes: the **Desert Village**.

Guarding an ancient sandstone sanctum sits **Eldrin, the Elemental Wizard**. An enigmatic scholar with an expansive white beard, Eldrin's robes mysteriously transmute to reflect whatever biome he inhabits. Eldrin welcomes you as an apprentice Element-Smith and presents your inaugural task: forging the **Weaponer**, a reinforced stone-and-iron workstation capable of binding primal catalysts into unpowered stencils.

---

### Chapter IV: Forging the Six Primal Weapons
Under Eldrin's tutelage and through expeditions across every corner of the world, you harvest catalysts to forge the six legendary weapons:
1. **Sunforged Scimitar**: Infused with Dry Heat (Desert Cactus); releases radiant *Solar Arc* projectiles and blazes brighter under noon sunlight.
2. **Frostwake Pick**: Infused with Snow (Glacial Snow Blocks); freezes rivers in your path and erupts in *Glacial Shockwaves* that entomb foes in permafrost.
3. **Rootbound Axe**: Infused with Tree (Dark Forest Dark Oak); siphons life upon strike and roots enemies into the earth with *Verdant Entanglement*.
4. **Tidebreaker Trident**: Infused with Ocean (Abyssal Kelp); grants aquatic propulsion through *Tidebreaker Dash* and encloses adversaries in suffocating hydro-vortices.
5. **Stormcaller Hammer**: Infused with Mountain (Highland Calcite); shatters stone with warhammer impacts and summons *Thunderous Cataclysms* of lightning.
6. **Ashen Rift Blade**: Infused with Nether Fire (Netherrack); slices through reality to blink via *Void Rift Teleports* and implodes space with *Spatial Collapse*.

---

### Chapter V: Trial of Sir Solvane, the Everliving Knight
Armed with the first triad of weapons, your waypoint leads to a grand marble and obsidian coliseum: the **Everliving Arena**.

At its center lies the **Everliving Altar**. Offering three elemental remnants—Dry Heat, Snow, and Tree—awakens **Sir Solvane, the Everliving Knight**. Clad in unyielding plate mail, Sir Solvane wields sweeping martial blade flourishes, explosive dashing thrusts, and radiant core pulses. As his armor fractures through multiple combat phases, his radiant core becomes exposed.

Upon landing the final strike, rather than falling in defeat, Sir Solvane triggers an instantaneous, catastrophic **dark particle shockwave**—a dense kinetic burst of squid ink, sculk souls, and sonic boom ripples—liberating his three core fragments and leaving behind an ancient gilded relic: the **Upgrade Key**.

---

### Chapter VI: The Upgrade Key & The Elemental Anvil
The **Upgrade Key** is an uncraftable artifact dropped exclusively upon Sir Solvane's demise.

Approaching a conventional anvil and striking it with the Upgrade Key causes the metal to surge with celestial energy. A shower of enchanting glyphs and radiant flares erupts as the mundane iron transfigures into the **Elemental Anvil**, a glowing block enveloped in shimmering animated glints with miniature arcane runes endlessly orbiting its perimeter.

Through the Elemental Anvil alone can the Element-Smith combine and affix the seven unique custom weapon enchantments (`Solar Blast`, `Dawn's Siphon`, `Spike Shockwave`, `Timber Bastion`, `Tidal Ram`, `Thunderous Descent`, `Cataclysmic Rift`). Normal anvils cannot handle these ancient enchantments and actively reject any attempt to forge them.

---

### Chapter VII: Clashing with Terrakon, the Seismic Colossus
With your arsenal fully enchanted, you descend into the subterranean core to confront the second ancient guardian: **Terrakon, the Seismic Colossus**.

Placing Ocean, Mountain, and Nether Fire offerings upon the **Colossus Altar** awakens the titan. Terrakon shatters the earth, tearing massive blocks directly out of the arena floor to hurl at you, executing bone-crushing tectonic ground slams that shake the screen, and summoning crystalline Colossus Minis to swarm intruders.

Utilizing your jetpack's aerial mobility and weapon abilities, you chip away at Terrakon's rocky shell. Upon his collapse, the titan gently restores every single fractured block in the arena through an automated terrain restoration sequence, yielding immense treasure caches of Netherite, diamonds, and crying obsidian alongside the final three Core fragments.

---

### Conclusion: Restoration of Equilibrium
With all six fragments retrieved from Sir Solvane and Terrakon, the Primal Elemental Core is reunited. You return to Aeron and Eldrin as the acknowledged **Grand Element-Smith of the Realm**, master of flight, commander of the six elements, and guardian of eternal balance.

# 2. Core Gameplay Mechanics & Systems

Each distinct mechanic and technical feature developed in the mod is detailed below:

### 1. Waypoint Guidance HUD
- **Trigger / Toggle**: Press `[G]` at any time to toggle the heads-up display.
- **Functionality**: Renders an on-screen objective banner displaying the current quest milestone, a real-time directional compass arrow pointing toward the target coordinates, and dynamic distance-in-meters counters with elevation indicators (↑ / ↓).

### 2. Weapon Description & Arsenal Codex GUI
- **Trigger / Toggle**: Press `[K]` to open the Weapon Detail Menu.
- **Functionality**: A dedicated interactive full-screen codex featuring real-time interactive 3D model previews of all six custom weapons, complete with lore summaries, stat panels (attack speed, damage, durability), passive traits, active ability controls, required crafting materials, and recommended enchantments.

### 3. Dedicated Jetpack Armor Slot
- **Architecture**: Injected into the player inventory through custom screen handler and container mixins.
- **Functionality**: Provides an independent equipment slot specifically for the Elemental Jetpack. Players never have to sacrifice their chestplate armor or defensive attributes to take flight.

### 4. Jetpack Flight & Glide Dynamics
- **Engine Control**: Toggle thruster ignition online or offline by pressing ``` ` ``` (Grave accent).
- **Propulsion**: Hold `[Space]` while airborne to spool thrusters, propelling the player at high velocity in the exact vector of their cursor.
- **Safety Descent**: Hold `[Sneak]` (Shift) while airborne to activate inertial glide dampers, arresting vertical velocity and guaranteeing 100% negation of fall damage upon touchdown.

### 5. Interactive Branching NPC Dialogue
- **Interaction**: Right-click quest NPCs (Aeron or Eldrin).
- **Functionality**: Freezes player movement into an immersive cinematic conversation interface. Features multi-choice response trees, custom sound cues for voice/dialogue progression, stateful memory tracking previous conversations, and camera focus shifts.

### 6. Biome-Adaptive NPC Appearance
- **Visuals**: Dynamic texture rendering on Eldrin the Elemental Wizard.
- **Functionality**: Whenever Eldrin is rendered, the renderer queries the world biome at his coordinates and dynamically swaps between tailored texture skins:
  - *Desert*: Sun-baked desert traveler garb with golden trim.
  - *Snow*: Thick fur-lined hooded winter coat with icy embroideries.
  - *Forest / Plains*: Earthy woodland scholar robes with organic vine motifs.
  - *Mountain*: Reinforced stone-grey mountaineer robes.
  - *Ocean*: Deep teal water-resistant coastal vestments.
  - *Nether*: Dark obsidian-and-crimson flame-retardant vestments.

### 7. Schematic Blueprint Maps
- **Item Type**: Specialized map items discovered in Skyforge ship chests.
- **Functionality**: Right-clicking an uninspected blueprint initializes and synchronizes high-contrast technical schematic illustrations directly onto the map canvas, detailing component assembly instructions accompanied by page-turn audio feedback.

### 8. The Weaponer Crafting Station
- **Block**: Custom crafting block built from Smooth Stone and Iron Ingots.
- **Functionality**: Operates with a dual-input infusion matrix: Slot 0 accepts a base item (Element Core or Unpowered Stencil), and Slot 1 accepts a biome catalyst or refined element, seamlessly crafting advanced components without traditional crafting table shapes.

### 9. The Six Primal Elements & Infusion
- **Crafting**: Created by infusing an **Element Core** with biome materials inside the Weaponer:
  - `Cactus` → **Dry Heat Element**
  - `Snow Block` → **Snow Element**
  - `Dark Oak Log` → **Tree Element**
  - `Kelp` → **Ocean Element**
  - `Calcite` → **Mountain Element**
  - `Netherrack` → **Fire Element**

### 10. Custom 3D GeckoLib & PlayerAnimator Weaponry
- **Rendering & Animation**: All six weapons feature fully modeled 3D GeckoLib assets accompanied by custom third-person combat sequences powered by `PlayerAnimationLib`.
- **Combat Behaviors**: Integrates charged projectiles (Solar Arc), ground slams with block wave physics (Glacial Shockwave, Thunderous Cataclysm), directional player dashing (Tidebreaker Dash), organic enemy ensnarement (Verdant Entanglement), and invulnerability teleports (Void Rift).

### 11. Custom Enchantment Infusion & Normal Anvil Restriction
- **The Seven Enchantments**: Unique combat enhancers specifically paired to each custom weapon: `Solar Blast`, `Dawn's Siphon`, `Spike Shockwave`, `Timber Bastion`, `Tidal Ram`, `Thunderous Descent`, and `Cataclysmic Rift`.
- **Enforcement Mixin**: `AnvilScreenHandlerMixin` intercepts `updateResult` and `canTakeOutput`. If an operation involves any custom enchantment on a vanilla anvil, the output is cleared and cost is locked to 0. Only the **Elemental Anvil** permits custom enchantment application.
- **Orbital Particle FX**: The Elemental Anvil actively computes trigonometric orbits in `randomDisplayTick`, emitting rings of orbiting `ENCHANT`, `GLOW`, and `PORTAL` particles.

### 12. Boss Summoning Altars & Boss Phase Engines
- **Altars**: `EverlivingAltarBlock` and `ColossusAltarBlock` act as multi-item ritual receptacles with floating item display renderers.
- **Boss Phase Engine**: Multi-tiered boss state machines featuring dynamic health thresholds, enrage timers, attack cooldown cycles, staggered states, core vulnerability periods, and custom boss bar tracking.

### 13. Arena Terrain Destruction & Regeneration
- **Destructive Physics**: Terrakon dynamically extracts block states from the arena floor, converting them into flying physics entities (`ThrownBlockEntity`) to bombard the player.
- **Automated Restoration**: The arena tracks original block coordinates and states in a spatial buffer. Upon boss defeat or encounter reset, the environment sequentially reconstructs itself, preserving the structure for future challenges.
